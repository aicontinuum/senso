How it works

We install it. It logs itself. You hear about it only when it matters.

1
A technician installs the sensors

One site visit. Battery-powered sensors go inside each unit; one small gateway plugs into a socket and joins your existing Wi-Fi or ethernet. No cabling to the fridges.

2
Readings log themselves every 15 minutes

Temperature and humidity for every unit, kept for two years, timestamped by the sensor, not by a person.

3
You get an alert when something drifts

An email the moment a unit leaves its safe range, after a grace period you set. It says which unit, how far out, and for how long.

4
Reports are ready with one click

Pick a unit and a month, and a signed compliance report comes out as a PDF: every reading, every breach, who acknowledged it. Print it or email it to the inspector while they are still standing there.

The problem

Four problems every business with a fridge or freezer already has

Staff hours spent writing down numbers

Someone walks to every unit, reads the dial, and fills in a sheet, several times a day, every day. That is paid time spent on paperwork instead of the actual work.

A door left open during a busy shift

Someone takes something out and does not push the door shut. The unit warms for hours and nobody notices until what is inside is already spoiled.

Stock lost to a failure nobody saw

A compressor stops at 1:40 in the morning. By the time the first person arrives, everything inside has been warm for hours and goes in the bin, along with the money spent on it.

An inspector who finds a gap in the records

MOPH and Baladiya inspectors can suspend a licence over missing or unreliable temperature records. A closure notice on the door costs more than anything inside the fridge.

Questions

Questions people ask us on the first call

How long do the sensors last?

The sensors are sealed, battery-powered units rated for several years of use, including inside freezers. Senso watches each battery level and schedules a visit to swap the sensor before it runs out, so your log never has a gap.

Will the signal reach inside a walk-in or a freezer?

Yes. EU868 radio is chosen for exactly this; it passes through insulated doors and kitchen walls where Wi-Fi and Bluetooth do not. The technician confirms every sensor is reporting before leaving.

Will an inspector accept the reports?

The report shows every reading with its timestamp, every breach, and who acknowledged it, then is signed by your manager. It contains everything a paper log does, and none of the gaps.

How long does installation take?

About an hour for a typical restaurant kitchen, and we work around service. Nothing is drilled and no unit is switched off.

Is there a contract?

The service is month to month, with 30 days notice to stop. Installation is a one-time fee paid at the start.
